LCW PROJESİ - DHCP VE DNS ÖRNEK KOMUTLAR

DHCP Scope Örneği:
VLAN10-YONETIM
Network: 192.168.10.0/24
Gateway: 192.168.10.1
DNS: 192.168.80.10

PowerShell örneği:

Add-DhcpServerv4Scope `
 -Name "VLAN10-YONETIM" `
 -StartRange 192.168.10.100 `
 -EndRange 192.168.10.200 `
 -SubnetMask 255.255.255.0

Set-DhcpServerv4OptionValue `
 -ScopeId 192.168.10.0 `
 -Router 192.168.10.1 `
 -DnsServer 192.168.80.10 `
 -DnsDomain "lcw.local"

DNS Zone:
lcw.local

DNS A Kaydı Örneği:

Add-DnsServerResourceRecordA `
 -Name "lcw-dc01" `
 -ZoneName "lcw.local" `
 -IPv4Address "192.168.80.10"

Add-DnsServerResourceRecordA `
 -Name "web-dmz" `
 -ZoneName "lcw.local" `
 -IPv4Address "192.168.90.10"
